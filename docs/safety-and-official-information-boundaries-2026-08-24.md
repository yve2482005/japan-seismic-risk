# Safety and Official Information Boundaries — 24 August 2026

## Earthquake safety content

The emergency guide will use concise, non-speculative safety advice drawn from the U.S. Geological Survey’s earthquake safety guidance: when indoors, stay there, use **Drop, Cover, and Hold On**, stay clear of windows and heavy furniture, and do not rush outside during shaking. When outdoors, move to an open area away from structures and power lines. After shaking, people should anticipate aftershocks, check for injuries and immediate hazards, and follow official emergency instructions. [1]

The guide will not diagnose injuries, replace emergency services, or make evacuation decisions for the user.

## Official tsunami boundary

Official tsunami information is separate from earthquake detection and model forecasts. The authoritative Japanese public information entry point is the JMA tsunami warnings/advisories map. [2] The map is a dynamic public webpage, but this project does not collect it automatically because the monitoring system’s approved live source is the USGS downloadable CSV and the project must not infer an official tsunami status from an earthquake magnitude or model.

The application must display **“No verified tsunami information available in this app”** unless a separately reviewed, permitted official information integration has been added. It must link users to the official JMA page and instruct people near the coast to follow official tsunami instructions.
