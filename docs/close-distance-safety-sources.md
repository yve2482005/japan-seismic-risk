# Close-Distance Safety Panel Sources

The conditional safety panel is a **display aid only**. It appears when the app's existing local, approximate straight-line distance is 100 km or less; it does not constitute an official warning, evacuation order, or risk prediction.

| Display item | Verified source | Use in the app |
|---|---|---|
| **119** for ambulance and fire | [U.S. Embassy & Consulates in Japan — Calling for Help](https://jp.usembassy.gov/services/calling-for-help/) | Emergency contact tile; directs people to call only for emergencies. |
| **110** for police | [U.S. Embassy & Consulates in Japan — Calling for Help](https://jp.usembassy.gov/services/calling-for-help/) | Emergency contact tile; directs people to call only for emergencies. |
| Remain calm, ensure safety first, follow official information | [Japan Tourism Agency — Safety Tips](https://www.jnto.go.jp/safety-tips/eng/index.html) | Concise earthquake safety reminder and official-information link. |
| During a strong or long-lasting coastal earthquake, evacuate to higher ground and follow tsunami warnings | [Japan Tourism Agency — Safety Tips](https://www.jnto.go.jp/safety-tips/eng/index.html) | Coast-specific safety reminder, framed conditionally and without inferring tsunami risk from the app's distance calculation. |

The Japan National Tourism Organization's [Staying Safe in Japan](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/emergencies/) page also advises remaining calm, following staff instructions, knowing evacuation routes, and heading for higher ground if on the coast during a large earthquake.
