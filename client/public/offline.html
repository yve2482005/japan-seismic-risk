OFFLINE EMERGENCY GUIDE

Earthquake safety

This page contains safety guidance only. Follow official emergency instructions and contact local emergency services when needed.

1. During shaking

If indoors, stay there: Drop, Cover and Hold On. Protect your head and neck, stay away from windows and heavy objects, and do not rush outside while shaking continues.

2. After shaking

Expect aftershocks. Check for injuries and immediate hazards, wear sturdy shoes around debris, and follow official instructions.

Tsunami

This offline guide cannot verify tsunami information. If you are near the coast, follow official tsunami instructions when available.

App boundary

Earthquake detections and any model output are not official warnings and are not exact predictions.
